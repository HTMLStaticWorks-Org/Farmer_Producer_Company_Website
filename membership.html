WHO CAN JOIN

MEMBERSHIP ELIGIBILITY, SHARE OWNERSHIP & VOTING RIGHTS

FARMVEST operates under the Companies Act as a Producer Company. Membership is open to all practicing agricultural producers residing within our operational districts.

Primary Producer Status: Must own or lease agricultural land and be actively engaged in cultivation.
Nominal Equity Share: Purchase of minimum 10 equity shares (nominal face value ₹100 each).
Documentation Required: Copy of land record (7/12 extract / Patta), Aadhaar card, and active bank passbook.
Democratic Rights: One Member, One Vote principle regardless of total acreage owned.

*Note: Detailed documentation and share allocation are finalized in accordance with the registered Articles of Association of the FPC.
