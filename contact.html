COMMON QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ABOUT FARMVEST

Find straightforward answers regarding commercial bulk procurement, delivery logistics, quality standards, and farmer collective membership.

Institutional buyers, retail supermarket chains, hotel chains, and exporters can submit a formal Request for Quotation (RFQ) through our online commercial desk or contact our wholesale desk directly at +91 (0) 80 2345 6789 / procurement@farmvest-fpc.demo. We provide customized commodity supply agreements, APEDA-certified grading specifications, recurring delivery schedules, and temperature-controlled logistics from our aggregation hubs.

For fresh perishables (vegetables, melons, and fruits), our standard MOQ for interstate wholesale shipments is 1 Metric Ton (MT) per commodity. For staple grains, pulses, and oilseeds, standard lots start at 5 MT. For regional wholesale mandis located within 250 km of our collection hubs, daily crated deliveries can be scheduled with 12 to 24 hours of advance notice.

Every batch aggregated across our farmer collectives undergoes automated multi-lane sizing, digital moisture calibration, and batch residue testing adhering to FSSAI and APEDA export norms. Consignments are packed in ventilated food-grade crates or multi-wall sacks with unique lot tracing barcodes connecting shipments directly to the origin farmer group.

Any cultivator with land records in our operational talukas can apply for cooperative membership. Simply visit your nearest village Farmer Interest Group (FIG) meeting or aggregation depot with your Aadhaar card, land patta (RTC/7-12) documents, and bank passbook. Members receive formal share certificates, digital weighment receipts, competitive farm-gate payments within 24 hours, and subsidized agricultural inputs.

Our central commercial headquarters is located at the APMC Central Agricultural Yard along the Pune – Bangalore National Corridor. We operate dedicated regional cold-chain pre-cooling and aggregation facilities across Nashik & Solapur (Maharashtra), Belagavi & Kolar (Karnataka), Chittoor & Guntur (Andhra Pradesh), and Indore & Raipur (Central India).
