FARM-TO-MARKET END-TO-END SERVICES

SUPPORTING EVERY STEP OF THE AGRICULTURAL SUPPLY CHAIN.

From pre-sowing soil agronomy and cluster harvest scheduling to cold-chain packing and multi-ton institutional distribution, FARMVEST delivers comprehensive agricultural business solutions.

FARMER SUPPORT & TRAINING

01. AGRONOMY ADVISORY, SOIL TESTING & FARMER TRAINING

Successful agricultural supply begins weeks before harvest. We organize smallholder producers into contiguous village clusters, aligning crop sowing dates with projected market demand.

Our dedicated agronomy officers conduct regular Farmer Field Schools, soil fertility profiling, integrated pest management guidance, and bio-input distribution to ensure uniform crop vigor across member farms.

Crop calendar synchronization to avoid seasonal supply gluts
Package of Practices (PoP) training for organic and residue-free yields
Subsidized micro-irrigation layout and seed procurement support
FARM-GATE COLLECTION

02. DIRECT FIELD HARVEST COLLECTION & DIGITAL WEIGHING

We eliminate unnecessary travel and distress selling by bringing certified procurement centers directly to the village level. Farmers deliver harvested crops to localized aggregation yards equipped with digital platform scales.

Each delivery is recorded on electronic handheld terminals, generating instant weighment receipts with moisture readings and direct-to-bank settlement records.

Decentralized aggregation points within 5km of member farm gates
Digital platform weighment with zero unauthorized deductions
Scheduled early-morning procurement runs to preserve crop freshness
QUALITY ASSURANCE & SORTING

03. COMPUTERIZED SORTING, CLEANING & CUSTOM PACKAGING

Every agricultural commodity collected undergoes systematic multi-stage inspection at our central packhouses. We separate produce into defined commercial tiers based on size, color uniformity, firmness, and cosmetic perfection.

From breathable plastic crates for short-haul vegetables to APEDA-compliant corrugated cartons for export fruits and spices, we follow strict food hygiene protocols.

Multi-grade optical and mechanical roller sizing
Digital moisture and brix sweetness testing
Custom export packaging with barcode traceability
COLD STORAGE & LOGISTICS

04. TEMPERATURE-CONTROLLED STORAGE & TRUCK DISPATCH

Post-harvest losses are minimized through scientific curing, pre-cooling, and humidity-managed warehousing. We coordinate dry godowns for non-perishable staples alongside temperature-controlled chambers for delicate horticultural produce.

Our transport coordination team schedules daily point-to-point freight dispatches to minimize in-transit vibration and thermal shock.

Well-ventilated onion and potato curing sheds to prevent decay
Pre-cooling rooms to extract field heat within hours of harvest
Strategic freight routing across major interstate transit highways
WHOLESALE MARKET ACCESS

05. DIRECT WHOLESALE BUYER CONTRACTS & TIMELY PAYMENTS

FARMVEST acts as a trusted, accredited single-window sourcing partner for commercial agricultural buyers across India. We aggregate fragmented farm output into truckload quantities that meet exact institutional criteria.

We work closely with retail chains, food processing companies, wholesale mandi merchants, and agricultural exporters, establishing seasonal contracts that provide price stability for both farmers and buyers.

Transparent forward supply agreements with guaranteed volume fulfillment
GST-compliant commercial invoicing and digital delivery challans
Custom grading and customized pallet packaging on demand
