SPECIALIZED FARMING REGIONS

8 SPECIALIZED REGIONAL CROP CLUSTERS ACROSS INDIA

Organized across distinct agro-climatic zones, each cluster specializes in crops suited to local rainfall, soil health, and traditional agronomic wisdom.

Dryland Cluster

Foxtail Millet (Kangni)

Aggregated from 1,400+ rainfed farmers in Bellary and Anantapur belts. High in calcium, low glycemic index, and chemical-free.

Coarse Grains

Pearl Millet (Bajra)

Cultivated by 2,200 smallholders across Marathwada and Northern Karnataka. Drought-resilient grain with rich iron content.

Dietary Grains

Barnyard Millet (Sanwa)

Grown by hilly tribal farming families. Exceptionally light, fast-digesting ancient grain ideal for dietary flour blends.

Indigenous Nutri-Cereals

Kodo & Little Millet

Sourced from traditional dryland collectives in Madhya Pradesh. Natural pest resistance requiring zero synthetic sprays.

Staple Grains

Parboiled Rice Cluster

Aggregated from Krishna-Godavari wetland producers. Parboiled in modern cooperative facilities to lock in vitamins.

Aromatic Rice

Basmati Milling Cluster

Specialized northern producer groups producing Traditional and 1121 Basmati with authentic geographical origin tracking.

Oilseeds & Spices

Mustard & Oilseeds Cluster

High-oil-content yellow and black mustard seed lots aggregated for cold-pressed kachi ghani oil millers across India.

Commercial Horticulture

Brassica Horticulture Cluster

Intensive vegetable growing belts producing snow-white cauliflower, cabbage, and broccoli with synchronized daily harvests.
