Orchard Horticulture & Export Grade

Premium Farm-Harvested Mangoes

Carefully harvested from certified producer clusters in Konkan, Dharwad, and Chittoor belts. Each batch is harvested at physiological maturity (85% maturity index) to allow natural ethylene ripening without synthetic calcium carbide.

Technical Sourcing Specifications

Varieties Available:
Alphonso, Kesar, Banganapalli, Totapuri
Brix Sweetness:
18° – 22° Brix at Full Ripeness
Sizing / Weight:
220g – 280g (Grade A) / 280g+ (Jumbo)
Post-Harvest Treatment:
Hot Water Dip (HWT) & Desapping
Packaging Options:
CFB Boxes (3.5kg / 5kg / 10kg) with Foam Nets
Minimum Order Qty:
500 kg (Domestic) / 2 MT (Export)
